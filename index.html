First launch

One extra step the first time.

Sfumo is made by an independent developer and isn't registered with Apple's paid developer programme, so macOS asks you to approve it once.

  1. Drag Sfumo into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. That's it. Look for the small window icon in your menu bar.
Questions

Good to know.

Does it close or move my windows?

No. Sfumo only places a soft overlay behind the window you're using. Turn it off and your desktop is exactly as you left it.

What permissions does it need?

None. It doesn't ask for Accessibility or Screen Recording access, and it never connects to the internet.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Apps from developers outside Apple's paid programme show that warning. You approve Sfumo once in System Settings › Privacy & Security, and the steps come with the download.
